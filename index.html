personal-assistant

personal-assistant is a private, personal Telegram bot used only by its owner and her household. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy policy

The app reads the owner's Gmail with read-only access (gmail.readonly) to send her a daily summary of new emails. It cannot send, change or delete email.

Email content is used only to create that summary. It is not sold, shared or used for any other purpose. Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/connections.
